About Noisecore

Vision To become an audio brand that unites sound quality, bold design, and the spirit of freedom. To create a listening experience that is personal, powerful, and full of character. To encourage self-expression through innovative and emotionally driven technology. To amplify the world in a way that is louder, more authentic, and deeply meaningful. Mission To deliver audio products with premium sound quality and design that reflects strong character. To create an audio experience that is not only heard, but emotionally felt. To continuously innovate in technology and aesthetics to suit a dynamic modern lifestyle. To build a community of listeners who value authenticity, freedom of expression, and sonic power. To voice the Noisecore spirit to the world—loud, clear, and full of meaning.

Our History

Founded in 2021, Noisecore grew from our passion for pure, powerful, and characterful sound. Inspired by the spirit of extreme music that defies boundaries, we built Noisecore as an audio brand that delivers an intense and authentic listening experience. Starting from a small space and big dreams, we designed our first product—a headphone that not only sounds powerful but also feels personal. Since then, Noisecore has continued to grow, offering earbuds and speakers that combine technology, design, and the spirit of freedom. We believe that sound is not just heard but felt. And Noisecore is our way of making the world hear louder.
